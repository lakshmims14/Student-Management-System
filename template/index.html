About Us

A Legacy of Knowledge & Innovation

Founded in 1998, Greenwood College has been at the forefront of higher education, nurturing thousands of students into accomplished professionals. Our NAAC-accredited institution combines academic rigour with practical learning to deliver a truly transformative experience.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, a dedicated faculty of subject-matter experts, and strong industry partnerships, we prepare every student not just for a career — but for life.
